Purpose: accepted final render
Referenced supporting inputs:
none

EXACT SUBMITTED PROMPT
Design one wall display interface for the fictional "Morrowfield Cooperative Laundry Status" concept. The concept is a cooperative laundry-room status display.

USER AND GOAL
The primary audience is operators and volunteers in the fictional Morrowfield Commons program. Make scan fictional machine states from across the room the unmistakable main action.

INFORMATION AND STRUCTURE
Use only WILLOW HOUSE LAUNDRY; Washer 1 AVAILABLE; Washer 2 18 MIN; Dryer 1 32 MIN; Dryer 2 OUT OF SERVICE; queue note Remove items within 10 min; maintenance notice Dryer 2 review Tuesday; not connected to equipment; concept only. Show machine states, estimated fictional cycle times, queue etiquette, and one maintenance notice at distance-readable scale. Keep hierarchy, states, and navigation legible.

VISUAL DIRECTION
Design for a 1440-pixel landscape desktop or wall-display concept with mouse or glance-scale interaction as appropriate. Use simple icons and no claim that displayed states are connected to real equipment. Apply near-white, deep slate, cyan, and warm orange with high state contrast and minimal decoration without borrowing a real product or brand system.

CONSTRAINTS
Treat every name, metric, status, and message as fictional or supplied. Do not imply functional testing, accessibility certification, legal compliance, medical advice, financial advice, or operational readiness. Add no real brand, unauthorized real-person likeness, signature, or watermark. Avoid cropped device frames, illegible or misspelled labels, duplicate controls, added screens, unsupported data, decorative stock photos, and text outside the supplied manifest.

OUTPUT
Return one 16:9 concept image with readable hierarchy and no unrequested screens.

END CALL 01
