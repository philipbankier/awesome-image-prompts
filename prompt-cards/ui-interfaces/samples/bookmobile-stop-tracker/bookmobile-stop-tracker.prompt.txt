EXACT SUBMITTED PROMPT
Design one mobile status interface for the fictional "Morrowfield Bookmobile Stop Tracker" concept. The concept is a fictional bookmobile stop tracker.

USER AND GOAL
The primary audience is operators and volunteers in the fictional Morrowfield Commons program. Make find the next fictional bookmobile stop and mark it as a favorite the unmistakable main action.

INFORMATION AND STRUCTURE
Use only MORROWFIELD BOOKMOBILE; Today; Cedar Square 10:00 / Planned; Lantern Court 11:30 / Planned; Willow Green 14:00 / Service note Short stop; route Cedar > Lantern > Willow; button FAVORITE STOP; schedule concept, not live tracking. Stack upcoming stops, an invented route strip, service notes, and a favorite-stop control. Keep hierarchy, states, and navigation legible.

VISUAL DIRECTION
Design for a 6.7-inch portrait mobile or kiosk concept with touch input and generous safe areas. Use cheerful civic colors and do not imply live tracking or guaranteed service. Apply paper white, charcoal, moss green, and muted ochre with generous spacing and sturdy rounded cards without borrowing a real product or brand system.

CONSTRAINTS
Treat every name, metric, status, and message as fictional or supplied. Do not imply functional testing, accessibility certification, legal compliance, medical advice, financial advice, or operational readiness. Add no real brand, unauthorized real-person likeness, signature, or watermark. Avoid cropped device frames, illegible or misspelled labels, duplicate controls, added screens, unsupported data, decorative stock photos, and text outside the supplied manifest.

OUTPUT
Return one 9:16 concept image with readable hierarchy and no unrequested screens.
