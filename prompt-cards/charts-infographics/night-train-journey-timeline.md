# Night Train Journey Timeline

## Use this when

Use this card when the itinerary, times, intervals, and milestones are fixed. It is a communication graphic, not a live timetable. Use a Recipe when exact text, time arithmetic, or repair requires structured evidence.

## Required inputs

Provide the fictional route title, ordered stops, exact departure and arrival times, time zone, intervals, milestone labels, icon vocabulary, palette, and disclaimer.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Lay out one wide horizontal timeline on {BACKGROUND} with {TITLE_EXACT} and a single uninterrupted reading path.
SUBJECT: Show the fictional overnight journey from {ORIGIN} to {DESTINATION} using the ordered events in {EVENT_SEQUENCE}.
DETAILS: Render {TIME_LABELS}, {STOP_LABELS}, {INTERVALS}, {MILESTONES}, and {DISCLAIMER_EXACT} exactly; use {ICON_SYSTEM} sparingly; apply {PALETTE}; and distinguish travel, dwell, and overnight periods using {ENCODING_SYSTEM}.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not invent stops, times, delays, fares, operators, or geographic claims. Preserve chronology and arithmetic, keep every label inside the canvas, and add no transport logo.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one legible {ASPECT_RATIO} timeline with enough spacing to verify every event.
```

## Variables

- `{TITLE_EXACT}`, `{ORIGIN}`, and `{DESTINATION}`: exact route strings.
- `{EVENT_SEQUENCE}`: complete ordered event manifest.
- `{TIME_LABELS}`, `{STOP_LABELS}`, and `{INTERVALS}`: exact values and units.
- `{MILESTONES}`: approved annotations.
- `{DISCLAIMER_EXACT}`: exact statement that the route and timetable are fictional.
- `{ICON_SYSTEM}`, `{PALETTE}`, and `{ENCODING_SYSTEM}`: non-brand visual grammar.
- `{BACKGROUND}` and `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: field and delivery crop.

## Negative constraints

No real rail brands, reordered stops, bad time arithmetic, invented delays, cropped labels, decorative maps, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Read the timeline end to end, recalculate every interval, compare every stop and time with the manifest, and confirm the disclaimer identifies the route as fictional.
