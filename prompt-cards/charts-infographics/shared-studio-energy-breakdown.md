# Shared Studio Energy Breakdown

## Use this when

Use this card to show a small, closed percentage breakdown with a declared comparison. It is not for forecasting, auditing, or deriving consumption data. Use a Recipe when exact values, scales, or repeatability must be demonstrated.

## Required inputs

Provide the exact title, fictional category values, total, units, comparison values, rounding rule, labels, palette, annotation, and source-owner approval.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Create one compact square chart card on {BACKGROUND} with {TITLE_EXACT} and a calm top-to-bottom hierarchy.
SUBJECT: Visualize the fictional shared-studio energy breakdown in {APPROVED_DATA} and the declared comparison in {COMPARISON_DATA}.
DETAILS: Use {PRIMARY_CHART} for the category shares, {SECONDARY_CHART} for the comparison, render {EXACT_LABELS}, {UNITS}, and {ANNOTATION_EXACT} verbatim, apply {ROUNDING_RULE} consistently, and use {PALETTE} with non-color reinforcement.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not recalculate, invent, omit, or merge categories. Do not use 3D perspective, area distortion, decorative appliances, or unsupported savings claims. Reinforce color with labels or patterns.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one balanced {ASPECT_RATIO} graphic whose totals and labels can be checked at full size.
```

## Variables

- `{TITLE_EXACT}` and `{ANNOTATION_EXACT}`: approved display strings.
- `{APPROVED_DATA}` and `{COMPARISON_DATA}`: complete fictional numeric inputs.
- `{PRIMARY_CHART}` and `{SECONDARY_CHART}`: declared chart forms.
- `{EXACT_LABELS}`, `{UNITS}`, and `{ROUNDING_RULE}`: data-display rules.
- `{PALETTE}`: approved chart colors.
- `{BACKGROUND}` and `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: presentation field and crop.

## Negative constraints

No totals that fail to reconcile, mismatched legends, distorted chart areas, invented savings, real utility marks, pseudo-text, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Recalculate the total, compare every label-value pair, verify rounding, and confirm both charts use the declared data rather than decorative proportions.
