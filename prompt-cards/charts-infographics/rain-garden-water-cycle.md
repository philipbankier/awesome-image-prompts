# Rain Garden Water Cycle

## Use this when

Use this card to explain one bounded rain-garden flow whose stages and relationships are already approved. It is not a substitute for engineering or environmental review. Use a Recipe when every fact, connector, and repair must be recorded.

## Required inputs

Provide the exact title, approved stage list, required directional relationships, short labels, cross-section elements, palette, reading order, conclusion, and prohibited claims.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Create one portrait educational infographic on {BACKGROUND}, arranged in the declared {READING_ORDER} beneath {TITLE_EXACT}.
SUBJECT: Explain the fictional rain-garden sequence {STAGE_LIST} using the approved cross-section elements in {REQUIRED_VISUALS}.
DETAILS: Connect stages only according to {REQUIRED_RELATIONSHIPS}, render {EXACT_LABELS} and {CONCLUSION_EXACT} verbatim, use {PALETTE}, and pair every colored flow with arrows or patterns.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not invent performance percentages, time spans, species, engineering claims, or causal steps. Keep arrows connected to their named endpoints and all text inside the canvas. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one clear {ASPECT_RATIO} process infographic that can be audited against the supplied fact list.
```

## Variables

- `{TITLE_EXACT}` and `{CONCLUSION_EXACT}`: approved exact strings.
- `{STAGE_LIST}`: ordered process stages.
- `{REQUIRED_RELATIONSHIPS}`: allowed directional connections.
- `{REQUIRED_VISUALS}`: approved cross-section objects.
- `{EXACT_LABELS}`: closed label manifest.
- `{READING_ORDER}`, `{PALETTE}`, `{BACKGROUND}`, `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: layout and delivery rules.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: additional prohibited claims or motifs.

## Negative constraints

No unsupported environmental claims, reversed arrows, disconnected labels, decorative pseudo-writing, real product marks, signatures, watermarks, or color-only encoding.

## Quick check

Trace every flow arrow, map each statement to an approved fact, transcribe every label, and fail the card if any new quantity, species, or claim appears.
