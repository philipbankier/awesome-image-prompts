# Courtyard Micro-Library

## Use this when

Use this card for an exterior architectural visualization of a small public reading pavilion organized around a planted courtyard. Use the linked [Recipe](../../recipes/courtyard-micro-library/RECIPE.md) when circulation, program, inspection, or evidence matters.

## Required inputs

- Site climate, neighborhood context, and approximate footprint.
- Program, occupancy, accessibility, and circulation requirements.
- Material palette, courtyard planting, time of day, camera view, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Visualize a {SITE_CONTEXT} at {TIME_AND_WEATHER}, with surrounding scale described by {NEIGHBORHOOD_EDGE}.

SUBJECT
Design one compact public micro-library organized around {COURTYARD_DESCRIPTION}, occupying approximately {FOOTPRINT}.

DETAILS
Include {PROGRAM}, an obvious accessible entrance, {CIRCULATION}, and {MATERIAL_PALETTE}. Use {PLANTING} to shape shade and privacy without blocking the entry.

CONSTRAINTS
Keep structure, spans, drainage, thresholds, guardrails, and human scale plausible. No impossible cantilevers, blocked doors, inaccessible steps, duplicated buildings, luxury-resort cues, logos, signage, watermarks, or unapproved text.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one legible architectural exterior at {ASPECT_RATIO} from {CAMERA_VIEW}, showing the building, courtyard relationship, entrance, and public character in one frame.
```

## Variables

- `{SITE_CONTEXT}`: climate, ground condition, and immediate setting.
- `{TIME_AND_WEATHER}`: light, sky, and surface conditions.
- `{NEIGHBORHOOD_EDGE}`: adjacent street, walls, trees, and building scale.
- `{COURTYARD_DESCRIPTION}`: size, shape, surface, and central use.
- `{FOOTPRINT}`: approximate enclosed and courtyard area.
- `{PROGRAM}`: reading room, shelves, desk, storage, and service spaces.
- `{CIRCULATION}`: public route from street through the building.
- `{MATERIAL_PALETTE}`: limited structural and finish materials.
- `{PLANTING}`: climate-appropriate courtyard planting.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: final width-to-height ratio.
- `{CAMERA_VIEW}`: declared exterior viewpoint and lens character.

## Negative constraints

- No inaccessible entrance, ambiguous circulation, sealed courtyard, or blocked glazing.
- No implausible structure, floating roof, missing drainage, or scale inconsistency.
- No people crowd, cars, brand signage, or decorative text unless approved.

## Quick check

Trace a step-free path from the public edge to the entrance, locate every requested program element, and inspect roof support, thresholds, and drainage for plausibility.
