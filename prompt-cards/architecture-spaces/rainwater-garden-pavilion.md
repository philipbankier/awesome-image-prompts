# Rainwater Garden Pavilion

## Use this when

Use this card for a public pavilion concept whose roof visibly directs rainwater into a planted garden. Use a Recipe when hydraulic performance, construction details, or compliance claims matter.

## Required inputs

- Site climate, soil, rainfall context, and fictional or authorized setting.
- Pavilion use, approximate footprint, roof and support concept.
- Visible water route, planting palette, materials, camera view, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Visualize a {SITE_CONTEXT} during {RAIN_CONDITION}, with wet surfaces and vegetation appropriate to {CLIMATE}.

SUBJECT
Show one small public {PAVILION_USE} pavilion with an approximate footprint of {APPROXIMATE_FOOTPRINT}, whose {ROOF_FORM} directs visible rainwater toward {GARDEN_DESCRIPTION}.

DETAILS
Use {STRUCTURE_AND_MATERIALS}, {PLANTING_PALETTE}, a clear accessible path, {SEATING_OR_PROGRAM}, and a legible water route from roof edge through {WATER_ELEMENTS} into the planted basin.

CONSTRAINTS
Keep water moving downhill, overflow routes plausible, structure grounded, and occupied areas protected. No impossible uphill flow, flooded access, unsupported roof, tropical plants in the wrong climate, people crowd, logos, watermarks, labels, or performance claims.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one explanatory but natural architectural scene at {ASPECT_RATIO} from {CAMERA_VIEW}, with pavilion use and rainwater movement understandable without text.
```

## Variables

- `{SITE_CONTEXT}`: park, courtyard, trail edge, or civic garden.
- `{RAIN_CONDITION}`: rainfall intensity, sky, and surface wetness.
- `{CLIMATE}`: regional climate and seasonal condition.
- `{PAVILION_USE}`: shelter, classroom, waiting, or gathering use.
- `{APPROXIMATE_FOOTPRINT}`: approved approximate dimensions or area.
- `{ROOF_FORM}`: slope, fold, gutter, and collection edge.
- `{GARDEN_DESCRIPTION}`: basin form, soil, stones, and planting zones.
- `{PLANTING_PALETTE}`: approved species, forms, and seasonal colors.
- `{STRUCTURE_AND_MATERIALS}`: supports, roof, paving, and finish palette.
- `{SEATING_OR_PROGRAM}`: exact public elements and count.
- `{WATER_ELEMENTS}`: scupper, chain, channel, runnel, or splash stone.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: final width-to-height ratio.
- `{CAMERA_VIEW}`: view that shows roof, route, basin, and entry.

## Negative constraints

- No engineering capacity, sustainability, flood-control, or code-compliance claim.
- No standing water across the accessible route or occupied floor.
- No hidden source, disconnected gutter, decorative water moving against gravity, or extra pavilion.

## Quick check

Trace the water route from roof to basin, confirm overflow avoids the public path, and inspect supports, slopes, and plant climate fit.
