# Saltmarsh Field Classroom

## Use this when

Use this card for a lightweight field classroom beside a fictional saltmarsh. It produces a concept visualization only, not a construction, structural, accessibility, code, permit, or safety claim. Use a Recipe when geometry preservation or evaluated iterations are required.

## Required inputs

- A fictional or authorized site and project context.
- The program, users, scale cues, climate, and site constraints.
- Approved materials, lighting, viewpoint, and landscape direction.
- The visual risks and unsupported claims to avoid.
- The attached input asset or assets, an authority statement, assigned roles, and preservation scope.

## Prompt

```text
Create one architectural exterior concept for {PROJECT_CONTEXT}. Visualize a lightweight field classroom beside a fictional saltmarsh.

SITE AND PROGRAM
Use {SITE_BRIEF} and support {PROGRAM} without inventing site facts or regulatory approval.

FORM AND COMPOSITION
Follow {VIEWPOINT_AND_LANDSCAPE}. Set a raised teaching deck beneath a shallow roof, with one boardwalk approach and framed wetland views. Keep circulation, scale cues, and spatial hierarchy visually coherent.

MATERIAL AND ATMOSPHERE
Use weathered timber, pale metal, filtered daylight, and visibly reversible foundations without claiming ecological approval. Apply {MATERIALS_AND_LIGHT} with physically plausible junctions and shadows.

AUTHORIZED SKETCH INPUT
Use {SKETCH_INPUT} only as an authorized guide for the declared arrangement. Preserve {PRESERVE_FEATURES}; do not copy a protected design language, named living artist style, signature, logo, or detail that the sketch does not establish.

CONSTRAINTS
This is an illustrative proposal. Do not claim structural feasibility, code compliance, accessibility approval, environmental performance, construction accuracy, or site permission. Add no real brand, real-person likeness, signature, or watermark. Avoid {MUST_AVOID}.

OUTPUT
Return one 16:9 architectural concept image with a clear viewpoint.
```

## Variables

- `{PROJECT_CONTEXT}`: the fictional or authorized project and setting
- `{SITE_BRIEF}`: the known site orientation, climate, terrain, and boundaries
- `{PROGRAM}`: the intended activities, users, capacity, and spatial priorities
- `{VIEWPOINT_AND_LANDSCAPE}`: the approved camera position, lens character, horizon, landscape, and foreground direction
- `{MATERIALS_AND_LIGHT}`: the approved material palette, weather, season, and lighting
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: unsupported claims, unwanted forms, and prohibited site details
- `{SKETCH_INPUT}`: the attached project-owned or otherwise authorized sketch
- `{PRESERVE_FEATURES}`: the layout, geometry, or relationships explicitly approved for preservation

## Negative constraints

- No construction, structural, code, accessibility, permit, or environmental-performance claim.
- No copied landmark, real brand, identifiable person, signature, or watermark.
- Do not contradict the spatial direction: Set a raised teaching deck beneath a shallow roof, with one boardwalk approach and framed wetland views.
