# Grain Shed Community Workshop

## Use this when

Use this card for an adaptive-reuse workshop inside a fictional or otherwise authorized grain shed. It produces a concept visualization only, not a construction, structural, accessibility, code, permit, or safety claim. Use a Recipe when geometry preservation or evaluated iterations are required.

## Required inputs

- A fictional or authorized site and project context.
- The program, users, scale cues, climate, and site constraints.
- Approved materials, lighting, viewpoint, and landscape direction.
- The visual risks and unsupported claims to avoid.
- The attached input asset or assets, an authority statement, assigned roles, and preservation scope.

## Prompt

```text
Create one adaptive-reuse interior concept for {PROJECT_CONTEXT}. Visualize an adaptive-reuse workshop inside a fictional or otherwise authorized grain shed.

SITE AND PROGRAM
Use {SITE_BRIEF} and support {PROGRAM} without inventing site facts or regulatory approval.

FORM AND COMPOSITION
Follow {VIEWPOINT_AND_LANDSCAPE}. Preserve the declared structural bays while inserting freestanding tool islands, a materials library, and a bright shared table. Keep circulation, scale cues, and spatial hierarchy visually coherent.

MATERIAL AND ATMOSPHERE
Keep old and new construction visually distinct, use honest timber and steel textures, and avoid copying a real protected building. Apply {MATERIALS_AND_LIGHT} with physically plausible junctions and shadows.

AUTHORIZED IMAGE SET
Use only {IMAGE_SET}, a small set whose items are all authorized for this task. Follow {IMAGE_ROLES} so each image has one declared purpose, and preserve only {PRESERVE_FEATURES}. Do not merge identities, brands, provenance, or hidden details across the sources.

CONSTRAINTS
This is an illustrative proposal. Do not claim structural feasibility, code compliance, accessibility approval, environmental performance, construction accuracy, or site permission. Add no real brand, real-person likeness, signature, or watermark. Avoid {MUST_AVOID}.

OUTPUT
Return one 4:3 architectural concept image with a clear viewpoint.
```

## Variables

- `{PROJECT_CONTEXT}`: the fictional or authorized project and setting
- `{SITE_BRIEF}`: the known site orientation, climate, terrain, and boundaries
- `{PROGRAM}`: the intended activities, users, capacity, and spatial priorities
- `{VIEWPOINT_AND_LANDSCAPE}`: the approved camera position, lens character, horizon, landscape, and foreground direction
- `{MATERIALS_AND_LIGHT}`: the approved material palette, weather, season, and lighting
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: unsupported claims, unwanted forms, and prohibited site details
- `{IMAGE_SET}`: the attached authorized image set
- `{IMAGE_ROLES}`: the explicit source role assigned to every image
- `{PRESERVE_FEATURES}`: the approved visual facts to retain from each source

## Negative constraints

- No construction, structural, code, accessibility, permit, or environmental-performance claim.
- No copied landmark, real brand, identifiable person, signature, or watermark.
- Do not contradict the spatial direction: Preserve the declared structural bays while inserting freestanding tool islands, a materials library, and a bright shared table.
