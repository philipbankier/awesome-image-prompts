# Cliffside Reading Room Interior

## Use this when

Use this card for one plausible interior visualization of a quiet reading room set against a cliff or steep coastal landscape. Use a Recipe for buildable detailing, code review, or evidence.

## Required inputs

- Fictional or authorized site orientation and view.
- Room dimensions, occupancy, entry, and furniture needs.
- Structure, materials, glazing strategy, light, weather, camera view, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Create the interior of a {ROOM_SIZE} reading room embedded beside {CLIFF_CONTEXT} during {TIME_AND_WEATHER}.

SUBJECT
Center the experience on {VIEW_ORIENTATION}, while keeping the entry and circulation visible from {CAMERA_VIEW}.

DETAILS
Use {STRUCTURE}, {MATERIAL_PALETTE}, {GLAZING}, and exactly {FURNITURE_MANIFEST}. Integrate {LIGHTING_STRATEGY} and a safe edge condition between the occupied room and the cliff.

CONSTRAINTS
Keep floor levels, spans, glazing thickness, drainage, furniture scale, and circulation plausible. No unsupported glass, impossible overhangs, blocked exits, floating furniture, fantasy caves, people, logos, watermarks, or unapproved text.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one calm interior visualization at {ASPECT_RATIO}, with readable spatial depth, grounded materials, and a strong but believable relationship to the cliff view.
```

## Variables

- `{ROOM_SIZE}`: approximate width, depth, and ceiling height.
- `{CLIFF_CONTEXT}`: rock, coast, vegetation, and exposure.
- `{TIME_AND_WEATHER}`: light and atmospheric conditions.
- `{VIEW_ORIENTATION}`: framed landscape direction and focal view.
- `{CAMERA_VIEW}`: camera location, height, and lens character.
- `{STRUCTURE}`: wall, roof, floor, and support strategy.
- `{MATERIAL_PALETTE}`: limited interior materials and finishes.
- `{GLAZING}`: opening size, frame, and shading treatment.
- `{FURNITURE_MANIFEST}`: exact furniture pieces and count.
- `{LIGHTING_STRATEGY}`: daylight and permitted fixtures.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: final width-to-height ratio.

## Negative constraints

- No missing guard, unsafe floor edge, blocked exit, or inaccessible route.
- No copied landmark interior, signature architect imitation, or impossible cliff geometry.
- No extra furniture, decorative clutter, candles, books with readable titles, or signage.

## Quick check

Verify the entry and exit path, count the furniture, inspect supports and glazing edges, and confirm the horizon and cliff sit outside rather than bleeding through the room.
