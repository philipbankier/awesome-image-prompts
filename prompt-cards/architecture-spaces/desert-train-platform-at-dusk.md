# Desert Train Platform at Dusk

## Use this when

Use this card for an atmospheric but plausible visualization of a small fictional desert rail platform at dusk. Use a Recipe when transport operations, safety, or site accuracy matter.

## Required inputs

- Fictional or authorized desert region, terrain, and weather.
- Platform type, shelter, track count, furnishings, and lighting.
- Camera position, dusk phase, palette, occupancy, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Visualize a {DESERT_CONTEXT} rail stop during {DUSK_CONDITION}, with {WEATHER} and a clear distant horizon.

SUBJECT
Show one modest {PLATFORM_TYPE} platform beside exactly {TRACK_COUNT} track, viewed from {CAMERA_VIEW}.

DETAILS
Include {SHELTER_DESCRIPTION}, {FURNISHING_MANIFEST}, {LIGHTING_ELEMENTS}, and {LANDSCAPE_DETAILS}, using {PALETTE} with occupancy set to {OCCUPANCY}. Keep the track, platform edge, access route, and shelter relationship physically coherent.

CONSTRAINTS
No train unless explicitly requested, no extra tracks, fantasy infrastructure, unsafe edge ambiguity, floating lamps, lush vegetation, urban skyline, crowds, brand signage, schedules, watermarks, or readable text.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one cinematic architectural scene at {ASPECT_RATIO}, balancing quiet desert atmosphere with a clearly legible transport platform.
```

## Variables

- `{DESERT_CONTEXT}`: terrain, geology, vegetation, and regional character.
- `{DUSK_CONDITION}`: sun position, sky gradient, and ambient light.
- `{WEATHER}`: wind, haze, cloud, and surface condition.
- `{PLATFORM_TYPE}`: side, island, raised, or ground-level platform.
- `{TRACK_COUNT}`: exact number of tracks.
- `{CAMERA_VIEW}`: location, height, and lens character.
- `{SHELTER_DESCRIPTION}`: roof, supports, bench, and material.
- `{FURNISHING_MANIFEST}`: exact objects and quantities.
- `{LIGHTING_ELEMENTS}`: fixture type, count, warmth, and placement.
- `{LANDSCAPE_DETAILS}`: approved plants, rocks, fences, or utility elements.
- `{PALETTE}`: approved dusk, landscape, structure, and light colors.
- `{OCCUPANCY}`: exact people count and placement, or `none`.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: final width-to-height ratio.

## Negative constraints

- No operational, accessibility, engineering, or safety-compliance claim.
- No mismatched rails, buried platform edge, disconnected access, or impossible perspective.
- No nostalgia branding, station name, advertisements, or decorative lettering.

## Quick check

Count the tracks and fixtures, trace the platform access, inspect rail perspective and edge continuity, and confirm the desert ecology matches the declared context.
