# Orchard Keeper Editorial Portrait

## Use this when

Use this card for a respectful portrait of one invented person in a fictional orchard. It is not for depicting a real worker, public figure, or supplied face. Use a Recipe when identity preservation, representation review, or repair needs evidence.

## Required inputs

Provide the fictional age range, facial features, body type, wardrobe, gesture, orchard setting, season, camera, light, palette, aspect ratio, and stereotype exclusions.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Create an editorial portrait in {FICTIONAL_ORCHARD_SETTING} during {SEASON}, photographed from {CAMERA_POSITION} under {LIGHT_DESCRIPTION}.
SUBJECT: Depict one fictional orchard keeper with {PERSON_DESCRIPTION}, wearing {WARDROBE} and performing {GESTURE}.
DETAILS: Preserve natural skin texture, believable hands, practical clothing wear, {PALETTE}, environmental depth, and a quiet expression that supports {EDITORIAL_MOOD}.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not resemble a real person or celebrity, add brand marks, romanticize hardship, exaggerate age or ethnicity, deform hands, or include signatures and watermarks. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one finished {ASPECT_RATIO} half-body portrait with clear eye focus and grounded environmental context.
```

## Variables

- `{FICTIONAL_ORCHARD_SETTING}` and `{SEASON}`: invented location and time.
- `{CAMERA_POSITION}` and `{LIGHT_DESCRIPTION}`: photographic framing.
- `{PERSON_DESCRIPTION}`: fictional identity features without a real reference.
- `{WARDROBE}` and `{GESTURE}`: exact clothing and visible action.
- `{PALETTE}` and `{EDITORIAL_MOOD}`: visual tone.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: additional stereotype exclusions.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: delivery crop.

## Negative constraints

No celebrity or real-person likeness, logos, stereotypes, caricature, glamorized hardship, malformed hands, excessive retouching, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Inspect face and hands, verify wardrobe and gesture, look for accidental marks, and reject stereotyped or overly polished treatment.
