# Community Kitchen Team Portrait

## Use this when

Use this card for one ensemble portrait of exactly four fictional volunteers. It is not for reconstructing a real group photograph or depicting named people. Use a Recipe when four-person identity, role, or representation review must be recorded.

## Required inputs

Provide four fictional identity descriptions, roles, wardrobe, positions, gestures, kitchen setting, prop inventory, palette, camera, light, aspect ratio, and representation review notes.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Compose an environmental team portrait in {FICTIONAL_KITCHEN_SETTING}, viewed from {CAMERA_POSITION} under {LIGHT_DESCRIPTION}.
SUBJECT: Depict exactly four fictional volunteers in {POSITION_ORDER}, each matching {IDENTITY_AND_ROLE_MANIFEST}.
DETAILS: Preserve each person's distinct face, body type, {WARDROBE_MANIFEST}, {GESTURE_MANIFEST}, and assigned item from {PROP_INVENTORY}; follow {REPRESENTATION_REVIEW_NOTES}; use {PALETTE} and natural interpersonal spacing.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not duplicate faces, swap roles, add people, use real logos, stereotype identities, resemble celebrities, deform hands, or include signatures and watermarks.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one finished {ASPECT_RATIO} group portrait in which all four people and roles remain distinct and fully visible.
```

## Variables

- `{FICTIONAL_KITCHEN_SETTING}` and `{CAMERA_POSITION}`: invented place and viewpoint.
- `{LIGHT_DESCRIPTION}` and `{PALETTE}`: lighting and color direction.
- `{POSITION_ORDER}` and `{IDENTITY_AND_ROLE_MANIFEST}`: exact four-person layout.
- `{WARDROBE_MANIFEST}` and `{GESTURE_MANIFEST}`: per-person clothing and pose.
- `{PROP_INVENTORY}`: one assigned item per role.
- `{REPRESENTATION_REVIEW_NOTES}`: approved identity and stereotype-avoidance guidance.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: delivery crop.

## Negative constraints

No real people, celebrity likenesses, repeated faces, fifth person, role swapping, logos, stereotypes, malformed hands, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Count four people, match every identity to role, wardrobe, gesture, position, and prop, then inspect faces and hands for duplication or deformation.
