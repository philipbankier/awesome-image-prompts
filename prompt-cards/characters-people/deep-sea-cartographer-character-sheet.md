# Deep-Sea Cartographer Character Sheet

## Use this when

Use this card for one three-view sheet of a wholly fictional character with fixed clothing and equipment. Use a Recipe when identity consistency and repair need structured evidence.

## Required inputs

Provide the fictional character's age range, body proportions, facial features, hair, clothing layers, equipment inventory, palette, front/side/back poses, labels, aspect ratio, and exclusions.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Build one neutral character-design sheet on {BACKGROUND}, divided into equal front, side, and back view regions.
SUBJECT: Show the same fictional deep-sea cartographer, {CHARACTER_DESCRIPTION}, once in each declared view.
DETAILS: Preserve {IDENTITY_ANCHORS}, {CLOTHING_LAYERS}, {EQUIPMENT_INVENTORY}, {PALETTE}, body proportions, handedness, and wear details across all three views; render only {VIEW_LABELS} as text.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not change age, face, hair, body type, costume, equipment count, or scale between views. Add no real insignia, celebrity likeness, franchise cue, signature, or watermark. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one finished {ASPECT_RATIO} turnaround sheet with three complete, aligned, equally scaled figures.
```

## Variables

- `{BACKGROUND}`: neutral sheet field.
- `{CHARACTER_DESCRIPTION}` and `{IDENTITY_ANCHORS}`: fictional identity and fixed recognition details.
- `{CLOTHING_LAYERS}` and `{EQUIPMENT_INVENTORY}`: exact wearable and prop manifests.
- `{PALETTE}`: approved character color system.
- `{VIEW_LABELS}`: exact front, side, and back strings.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: additional project-specific exclusions.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: delivery crop.

## Negative constraints

No celebrity likeness, real uniform or brand, franchise design language, identity drift, extra limbs, missing equipment, inconsistent scale, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Compare face, silhouette, clothing seams, equipment placement, proportions, handedness, and scale across all three views.
