# Solar-Sail Courier Action Pose

## Use this when

Use this card for a dynamic pose study of an original science-fiction courier. It is not for extending a franchise, copying recognizable armor, or matching a real performer. Use a Recipe when identity, equipment, or pose repair needs run evidence.

## Required inputs

Provide the fictional courier identity, silhouette, clothing, equipment, motion, direction of travel, environment, camera, light, palette, aspect ratio, and exclusions.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Set a wide action frame in {FICTIONAL_ENVIRONMENT}, viewed from {CAMERA_POSITION} with {LIGHT_DESCRIPTION} defining the movement path.
SUBJECT: Show one original solar-sail courier, {CHARACTER_DESCRIPTION}, executing {ACTION_POSE} toward {TRAVEL_DIRECTION}.
DETAILS: Preserve {SILHOUETTE}, {CLOTHING_MANIFEST}, {EQUIPMENT_INVENTORY}, {PALETTE}, readable limb overlap, believable balance, and a clear relationship between the courier and {SOLAR_SAIL_DEVICE}.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not add franchise symbols, known armor shapes, celebrity likenesses, extra limbs, duplicated equipment, impossible joints, text, signatures, or watermarks. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one finished {ASPECT_RATIO} action pose with an immediate direction, readable body mechanics, and complete equipment.
```

## Variables

- `{FICTIONAL_ENVIRONMENT}` and `{CAMERA_POSITION}`: original setting and viewpoint.
- `{LIGHT_DESCRIPTION}` and `{PALETTE}`: lighting and color system.
- `{CHARACTER_DESCRIPTION}` and `{SILHOUETTE}`: fictional identity and fixed form.
- `{ACTION_POSE}` and `{TRAVEL_DIRECTION}`: motion description.
- `{CLOTHING_MANIFEST}`, `{EQUIPMENT_INVENTORY}`, and `{SOLAR_SAIL_DEVICE}`: exact design inventory.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: additional project-specific exclusions.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: delivery crop.

## Negative constraints

No franchise cues, real space-agency marks, celebrity likenesses, extra limbs, broken anatomy, duplicated gear, motion blur that hides the pose, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Trace the pose and direction, count limbs and equipment, inspect the sail connection, and reject any recognizable franchise or real-person resemblance.
