# Night-Shift Tram Conductor

## Use this when

Use this card for one full-body design of a fictional transit worker in an invented setting. It is not for copying a real operator's uniform or authority insignia. Use a Recipe when costume, equipment, or identity consistency requires recorded inspection.

## Required inputs

Provide fictional identity traits, uniform pieces, original insignia geometry, tool inventory, stance, tram setting, palette, light, aspect ratio, and exclusions.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Stage a full-body character design beside {FICTIONAL_TRAM_SETTING} at night under {LIGHT_DESCRIPTION}.
SUBJECT: Depict one fictional tram conductor with {CHARACTER_DESCRIPTION}, standing in {STANCE}.
DETAILS: Render exactly {UNIFORM_PIECES}, {ORIGINAL_INSIGNIA}, and {TOOL_INVENTORY}; use {PALETTE}, practical fabric construction, visible footwear, believable hands, and a complete silhouette.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not use a real transit logo, military or police insignia, celebrity likeness, franchise uniform, extra tools, cropped feet, signature, or watermark. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one finished {ASPECT_RATIO} full-body design whose role is readable without real-world affiliation.
```

## Variables

- `{FICTIONAL_TRAM_SETTING}` and `{LIGHT_DESCRIPTION}`: invented environment and lighting.
- `{CHARACTER_DESCRIPTION}` and `{STANCE}`: fictional identity and pose.
- `{UNIFORM_PIECES}`: exact clothing manifest.
- `{ORIGINAL_INSIGNIA}`: project-authored non-authority symbol.
- `{TOOL_INVENTORY}` and `{PALETTE}`: exact props and color system.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: additional project-specific exclusions.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: delivery crop.

## Negative constraints

No real operator identity, police or military cues, celebrity likeness, franchise styling, cropped anatomy, extra tools, logos, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Count uniform pieces and tools, inspect hands and feet, confirm the insignia is original, and reject any recognizable real operator or authority cue.
