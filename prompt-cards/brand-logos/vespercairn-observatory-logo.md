# Vespercairn Observatory Logo

## Use this when

Use this card for one original symbol-and-wordmark lockup for the fictional Vespercairn Observatory. Use the linked [Recipe](../../recipes/vespercairn-observatory-logo/RECIPE.md) when exact wordmark rendering, small-size inspection, repair, or evidence matters.

## Required inputs

- Exact fictional organization name and optional descriptor.
- Symbol concept, construction logic, typography character, palette, and lockup orientation.
- Background, minimum-size intent, aspect ratio, and prohibited motifs.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Create one clean identity presentation on {BACKGROUND}, without stationery, signage, merchandise, or environmental mockups.

SUBJECT
Design an original symbol-and-wordmark lockup for {NAME_EXACT}, combining {CAIRN_GEOMETRY} with {OBSERVATORY_MOTIF} through one simple shared construction.

DETAILS
Use {LOCKUP_ORIENTATION}, {WORDMARK_DIRECTION}, {LINE_AND_SHAPE_RULES}, and {PALETTE}. Render only {EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}; keep the symbol recognizable in one color and at {MINIMUM_SIZE_INTENT}.

CONSTRAINTS
Use clean vector-like edges, balanced spacing, stable geometry, and no more than {COLOR_COUNT} colors. No real observatory identity, copied constellation mark, generic swoosh, stock telescope silhouette, gradient effect unless declared, mockup, extra logo option, signature, watermark, or unapproved text. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one centered 1:1 identity sheet at {OUTPUT_SIZE}, showing a single primary lockup with an original symbol and exact wordmark.
```

## Variables

- `{BACKGROUND}`: plain presentation field.
- `{NAME_EXACT}`: exact fictional wordmark.
- `{CAIRN_GEOMETRY}`: abstract stacked-stone construction.
- `{OBSERVATORY_MOTIF}`: one sky, aperture, horizon, or orbital cue.
- `{LOCKUP_ORIENTATION}`: horizontal, vertical, or compact arrangement.
- `{WORDMARK_DIRECTION}`: project-owned letterform character.
- `{LINE_AND_SHAPE_RULES}`: stroke, corner, symmetry, and negative-space logic.
- `{PALETTE}`: exact approved colors.
- `{EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}`: complete allowed text list.
- `{MINIMUM_SIZE_INTENT}`: intended small-use dimension.
- `{COLOR_COUNT}`: maximum color count.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: additional prohibited motifs.
- `{OUTPUT_SIZE}`: final pixel dimensions.

## Negative constraints

- No copied astronomy institution mark, national-space-agency cue, existing constellation logo, or trademarked symbol.
- No misspelled wordmark, extra descriptor, duplicated lockup, malformed letter, or illegible negative space.
- No photorealistic rocks, lens flare, star-field clutter, stationery scene, signature, or watermark.

## Quick check

Read the wordmark character by character, reduce the design to thumbnail size, imagine it in one color, and confirm the cairn and observatory cues form one symbol rather than two unrelated icons.
