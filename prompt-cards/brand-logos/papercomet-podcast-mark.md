# Papercomet Podcast Mark

## Use this when

Use this card for one square podcast cover mark that combines folded paper, a comet path, and the exact fictional name Papercomet. Use a Recipe for platform delivery, trademark review, or repeatability evidence.

## Required inputs

- Exact fictional podcast name and optional short descriptor.
- Paper-comet symbol logic, type direction, palette, and background.
- Small-size target, output size, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Create one square podcast mark on {BACKGROUND}, without headphones, microphones, platform chrome, or device mockups.

SUBJECT
Design an original symbol and exact wordmark for {NAME_EXACT}, turning {PAPER_FORM} into {COMET_MOTION} through one continuous visual idea.

DETAILS
Use {SYMBOL_CONSTRUCTION}, {WORDMARK_DIRECTION}, {PALETTE}, and {LOCKUP}. Render only {EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}; keep the title readable at {MINIMUM_SIZE_INTENT}.

CONSTRAINTS
Keep folds, tail direction, negative space, and letter spacing simple and coherent. No real podcast identity, platform logo, stock paper-plane icon, generic microphone, copied space mark, episode text, mockup, signature, watermark, or unapproved copy.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one centered 1:1 podcast cover mark at {OUTPUT_SIZE}, with a memorable paper-comet symbol and a strong small-size wordmark.
```

## Variables

- `{BACKGROUND}`: plain or subtly textured field.
- `{NAME_EXACT}`: exact fictional podcast title.
- `{PAPER_FORM}`: folded sheet, torn strip, or paper curl.
- `{COMET_MOTION}`: direction, arc, and tail behavior.
- `{SYMBOL_CONSTRUCTION}`: shape, stroke, and negative-space rules.
- `{WORDMARK_DIRECTION}`: project-owned lettering character.
- `{PALETTE}`: exact approved colors.
- `{LOCKUP}`: symbol-to-wordmark arrangement.
- `{EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}`: complete allowed text list.
- `{MINIMUM_SIZE_INTENT}`: smallest intended cover display.
- `{OUTPUT_SIZE}`: final pixel dimensions.

## Negative constraints

- No real podcast, media network, streaming platform, or publisher branding.
- No unreadable title, second symbol, literal rocket, audio waveform, or stock paper plane.
- No episode number, review badge, UI frame, signature, or watermark.

## Quick check

Read the title at thumbnail size, confirm the paper and comet cues share one silhouette, and verify there is no platform or microphone shorthand.
