# Bramblesignal Refill Shop Emblem

## Use this when

Use this card for one original emblem for a fictional neighborhood refill shop, combining a bramble branch and a reusable-container signal. Use a Recipe for trademark clearance, production artwork, or evidence.

## Required inputs

- Exact fictional shop name and optional short descriptor.
- Emblem shape, botanical cue, refill cue, palette, and print method.
- Wordmark placement, background, output size, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Create one flat emblem presentation on {BACKGROUND}, without storefront, packaging, or merchandise mockups.

SUBJECT
Design an original emblem for {NAME_EXACT}, merging {BRAMBLE_CUE} and {REFILL_CUE} inside {EMBLEM_SHAPE}.

DETAILS
Use {WORDMARK_DIRECTION}, {LINE_RULES}, {PALETTE}, and {PRINT_CONSTRAINT}. Render only {EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}; keep the emblem readable in one ink and at {MINIMUM_SIZE_INTENT}.

CONSTRAINTS
Keep thorn count, leaf rhythm, container geometry, and negative space controlled. No recycling trademark, certification mark, real shop identity, stock eco icon, copied crest, excessive leaves, photorealism, mockup, logo sheet, signature, watermark, or unapproved text.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one centered 1:1 brand emblem at {OUTPUT_SIZE}, with a compact silhouette and a clear reuse cue that does not rely on a generic recycling symbol.
```

## Variables

- `{BACKGROUND}`: plain light or dark field.
- `{NAME_EXACT}`: exact fictional shop name.
- `{BRAMBLE_CUE}`: approved branch, thorn, berry, or leaf geometry.
- `{REFILL_CUE}`: reusable jar, bottle, loop, or measured pour cue.
- `{EMBLEM_SHAPE}`: circle, shield, arch, or custom container.
- `{WORDMARK_DIRECTION}`: project-owned lettering character and placement.
- `{LINE_RULES}`: stroke weight, joins, symmetry, and detail limit.
- `{PALETTE}`: exact approved colors.
- `{PRINT_CONSTRAINT}`: one-color, stamp, screen print, or embroidery limit.
- `{EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}`: complete allowed text list.
- `{MINIMUM_SIZE_INTENT}`: smallest intended use.
- `{OUTPUT_SIZE}`: final pixel dimensions.

## Negative constraints

- No protected recycling, organic, fair-trade, or certification symbol.
- No real retailer name, copied eco-brand identity, generic leaf-only mark, or impossible container.
- No misspelled wordmark, extra tagline, gradient noise, mockup, signature, or watermark.

## Quick check

Read the name, inspect the silhouette at thumbnail size, and confirm the refill idea survives without a protected recycling symbol or color dependence.
