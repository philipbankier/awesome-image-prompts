# Emberferry Trail Club Badge

## Use this when

Use this card for one original embroidered badge for a fictional trail club, combining a warm ferry crossing with a simple path motif. Use a Recipe for real club identity, embroidery production testing, or evidence.

## Required inputs

- Exact fictional club name, region cue, and optional founding year.
- Badge shape, trail and ferry motif, thread palette, and stitch limits.
- Exact text, output size, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Create one flat embroidered-badge design on {BACKGROUND}, with no jacket, backpack, hand, or product mockup.

SUBJECT
Design an original badge for {NAME_EXACT}, combining {REGION_CUE}, {TRAIL_MOTIF}, {FERRY_MOTIF}, and {EMBER_CUE} inside {BADGE_SHAPE}.

DETAILS
Use {THREAD_PALETTE}, {STITCH_AND_LINE_RULES}, and {TYPE_PLACEMENT}. Render only {EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}; keep every shape suitable for {MINIMUM_STITCH_WIDTH} and the silhouette readable at {FINAL_BADGE_SIZE}.

CONSTRAINTS
Use flat thread colors, closed shapes, and controlled detail. No real club, park, ferry operator, protected trail emblem, national flag, copied patch, photorealistic landscape, mockup, signature, watermark, or unapproved text.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one centered 1:1 badge artwork at {OUTPUT_SIZE}, with a compact trail-crossing story and clear embroidery-ready separations.
```

## Variables

- `{BACKGROUND}`: plain presentation field.
- `{NAME_EXACT}`: exact fictional club name.
- `{REGION_CUE}`: approved fictional terrain, waterway, or geographic abstraction.
- `{TRAIL_MOTIF}`: path, switchback, or trail marker geometry.
- `{FERRY_MOTIF}`: simplified boat, wake, or crossing cue.
- `{EMBER_CUE}`: restrained sun, glow, or warm horizon shape.
- `{BADGE_SHAPE}`: circle, rounded shield, arch, or custom outline.
- `{THREAD_PALETTE}`: limited thread-color list.
- `{STITCH_AND_LINE_RULES}`: minimum line, gap, and fill behavior.
- `{TYPE_PLACEMENT}`: exact wordmark arc or baseline.
- `{EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}`: complete allowed text list.
- `{MINIMUM_STITCH_WIDTH}`: production-minded detail threshold.
- `{FINAL_BADGE_SIZE}`: intended physical dimensions.
- `{OUTPUT_SIZE}`: final pixel dimensions.

## Negative constraints

- No protected park, trail, ferry, civic, scouting, or outdoor-brand symbol.
- No tiny interior line, isolated stitch island, gradient, illegible curved text, or excess color.
- No apparel mockup, realistic fabric wrinkles, merrowed-edge photograph, signature, or watermark.

## Quick check

Count the thread colors, inspect every gap and line at the intended badge size, read the curved name, and confirm the ferry, trail, and ember cues remain distinct.
