# Larkglass Monogram

## Use this when

Use this card for one original `LG` monogram balancing a birdlike lift with a glass-cut geometric structure for the fictional Larkglass name. Use a Recipe for trademark review, final vector construction, or evidence.

## Required inputs

- Exact initials and fictional brand context.
- Monogram construction, stroke behavior, symmetry, palette, and background.
- Minimum-size intent, output size, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Create one flat monogram presentation on {BACKGROUND}, with no product, stationery, sign, or environmental mockup.

SUBJECT
Construct the exact initials {INITIALS_EXACT} for {BRAND_CONTEXT} as one original interlocking mark using {LARK_CUE} and {GLASS_GEOMETRY}.

DETAILS
Apply {CONSTRUCTION_GRID}, {STROKE_AND_CORNER_RULES}, {NEGATIVE_SPACE_RULES}, and {PALETTE}. Preserve both initials while keeping the combined silhouette recognizable at {MINIMUM_SIZE_INTENT}.

CONSTRAINTS
Use no other letter, word, bird illustration, crystal photograph, copied luxury monogram, heraldic crown, generic diamond, mockup, multiple logo options, signature, watermark, or decorative pseudo-text.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one centered 1:1 monogram at {OUTPUT_SIZE}, with crisp vector-like edges, balanced counters, and equal visual ownership of both initials.
```

## Variables

- `{BACKGROUND}`: plain presentation color.
- `{INITIALS_EXACT}`: exact two-letter monogram.
- `{BRAND_CONTEXT}`: fictional industry, audience, and intended brand character.
- `{LARK_CUE}`: abstract upward wing, beak, or flight rhythm.
- `{GLASS_GEOMETRY}`: facet, cut, refraction, or pane logic expressed as flat geometry.
- `{CONSTRUCTION_GRID}`: proportions and alignment system.
- `{STROKE_AND_CORNER_RULES}`: weight, joins, terminals, and corner treatment.
- `{NEGATIVE_SPACE_RULES}`: counters and separation between initials.
- `{PALETTE}`: one or two approved colors.
- `{MINIMUM_SIZE_INTENT}`: smallest intended use.
- `{OUTPUT_SIZE}`: final pixel dimensions.

## Negative constraints

- No existing fashion, hospitality, glassware, or jewelry monogram reference.
- No unreadable initials, accidental third letter, asymmetric weight, or fragile hairline detail.
- No photorealistic glass, metallic mockup, paper embossing scene, signature, or watermark.

## Quick check

Identify `L` and `G` independently, reduce the mark to a small single-color silhouette, and confirm neither initial disappears or becomes an unintended symbol.
