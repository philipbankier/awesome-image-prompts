# Gouache Night Market Fable

## Use this when

Use this card for one fictional picture-book scene with a clear focal exchange and original setting. It is not for reproducing a real market, culture-specific festival, or published book style. Use a Recipe when character continuity, cultural review, or repair must be recorded.

## Required inputs

Provide the fictional market setting, focal characters, exact stall and prop inventory, story action, palette, lighting, camera view, aspect ratio, and cultural exclusions.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Paint an original gouache night market in {FICTIONAL_SETTING}, viewed from {CAMERA_VIEW} beneath {LIGHTING_DESCRIPTION}.
SUBJECT: Focus on {FOCAL_CHARACTERS} performing {STORY_ACTION} at {PRIMARY_STALL}.
DETAILS: Include exactly {PROP_INVENTORY}, use {PALETTE}, opaque brush shapes, matte paper texture, simplified perspective, and small pools of warm and cool light that guide attention to the exchange.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not add readable brands, real flags, sacred symbols, stereotyped costume, celebrity likenesses, signatures, watermarks, or a named illustrator's style. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one finished {ASPECT_RATIO} story illustration with a clear focal action and secondary market rhythm.
```

## Variables

- `{FICTIONAL_SETTING}` and `{CAMERA_VIEW}`: invented location and viewpoint.
- `{LIGHTING_DESCRIPTION}` and `{PALETTE}`: approved light and color system.
- `{FOCAL_CHARACTERS}` and `{STORY_ACTION}`: fictional participants and visible beat.
- `{PRIMARY_STALL}` and `{PROP_INVENTORY}`: exact environmental content.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: additional cultural exclusions.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: delivery crop.

## Negative constraints

No real brands, copied market layouts, culture mashups, stereotyped clothing, celebrity likenesses, pseudo-signage, signatures, watermarks, or living-artist imitation.

## Quick check

Identify the focal action without explanation, count the supplied props, inspect cultural details for unsupported mixing, and reject readable unapproved signage.
