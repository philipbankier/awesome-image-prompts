# Two-Color Mountain Weather Linocut

## Use this when

Use this card for an original relief-print image whose weather and mountain silhouette must read through two inks. It is not for imitating a named printmaker. Use a Recipe when ink limits, composition, and repair need recorded inspection.

## Required inputs

Provide the fictional mountain profile, weather event, foreground elements, two ink colors, paper color, mark vocabulary, light direction, aspect ratio, and exclusions.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Compose a fictional mountain weather scene on {PAPER_COLOR}, viewed from {VIEWPOINT} with {WEATHER_EVENT} crossing the frame.
SUBJECT: Make {MOUNTAIN_PROFILE} the dominant silhouette, supported by exactly {FOREGROUND_ELEMENTS}.
DETAILS: Use only {INK_COLOR_ONE} and {INK_COLOR_TWO}, with {MARK_VOCABULARY} to separate sky, rock, snow, and vegetation; follow {LIGHT_DIRECTION}; preserve intentional carved gaps and slight handmade registration character.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not add gradients, photographic texture, a third ink, text, logos, signatures, recognizable real landmarks, or the style of any named artist. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one original {ASPECT_RATIO} two-color linocut-style illustration with strong value separation at thumbnail size.
```

## Variables

- `{PAPER_COLOR}` and `{VIEWPOINT}`: print field and camera position.
- `{WEATHER_EVENT}` and `{MOUNTAIN_PROFILE}`: fictional scene anchors.
- `{FOREGROUND_ELEMENTS}`: exact supporting-object list.
- `{INK_COLOR_ONE}` and `{INK_COLOR_TWO}`: the only inks.
- `{MARK_VOCABULARY}`: allowed hatch, gouge, and solid-fill treatments.
- `{LIGHT_DIRECTION}`: approved direction of light and shadow.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: additional project-specific exclusions.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: output crop.

## Negative constraints

No third color, gradients, photorealism, famous peak, copied composition, named-artist imitation, signatures, watermarks, pseudo-text, or muddy value structure.

## Quick check

Confirm only two inks appear, identify the weather at thumbnail size, inspect the mountain silhouette, and reject photographic or derivative details.
