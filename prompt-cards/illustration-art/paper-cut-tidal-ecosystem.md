# Paper-Cut Tidal Ecosystem

## Use this when

Use this card for one original layered-paper illustration with a closed subject inventory and declared depth order. Use a Recipe when layer preservation, repeatability, or repair evidence matters.

## Required inputs

Provide the fictional tide-pool setting, exact organism inventory, layer order, paper textures, palette, light direction, crop, aspect ratio, and exclusions.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Build a hand-cut paper diorama of {TIDE_POOL_SETTING} on {BACKGROUND}, viewed from {CAMERA_VIEW} under {LIGHT_DIRECTION}.
SUBJECT: Center the original tidal ecosystem containing exactly {ORGANISM_INVENTORY} arranged according to {LAYER_ORDER}.
DETAILS: Use {PAPER_TEXTURES}, {PALETTE}, crisp cut edges, small physical gaps between layers, coherent cast shadows, and believable overlap at each declared depth.
CONSTRAINTS: Keep every organism recognizable and separate. Do not add text, real logos, signatures, plastic-looking surfaces, photographic water, extra species, or motifs associated with a named artist. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one finished {ASPECT_RATIO} editorial illustration with a complete silhouette and inspectable layer separation.
```

## Variables

- `{TIDE_POOL_SETTING}` and `{BACKGROUND}`: fictional environment and backing field.
- `{CAMERA_VIEW}` and `{LIGHT_DIRECTION}`: viewpoint and shadow direction.
- `{ORGANISM_INVENTORY}`: exact subject list and counts.
- `{LAYER_ORDER}`: back-to-front depth manifest.
- `{PAPER_TEXTURES}` and `{PALETTE}`: approved material and color system.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: additional project-specific exclusions.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: delivery crop.

## Negative constraints

No fused layers, contradictory shadows, extra limbs or shells, plastic sheen, photographic textures, real brands, signatures, watermarks, or living-artist imitation.

## Quick check

Count every organism, trace the declared back-to-front order, verify one light direction, and reject fused edges or photographic material behavior.
