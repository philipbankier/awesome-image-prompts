# Geometric Quilt of Four Seasons

## Use this when

Use this card for one original square quilt composition with four declared seasonal regions. It is not for reproducing a traditional community-owned pattern or existing textile design. Use a Recipe when repeat structure, accessibility, or motif screening requires recorded review.

## Required inputs

Provide the grid, four seasonal motif sets, quadrant order, palette, seam width, symmetry rule, non-color distinctions, border treatment, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Present one flat square quilt face on {BACKGROUND}, centered and fully visible with {BORDER_TREATMENT}.
SUBJECT: Divide the quilt into {GRID_SPEC} and express the four seasons in {QUADRANT_ORDER} using only {SEASONAL_MOTIF_SETS}.
DETAILS: Apply {PALETTE}, {SEAM_WIDTH}, {SYMMETRY_RULE}, and {NON_COLOR_DISTINCTIONS}; keep every patch edge clean, every repeated unit intentional, and the four regions visually related but distinct.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not reproduce a named quilt block, community-specific ceremonial motif, real logo, text, signature, watermark, gradient, or photographic fabric scene.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one original {ASPECT_RATIO} geometric quilt design shown as a flat, inspectable composition.
```

## Variables

- `{BACKGROUND}` and `{BORDER_TREATMENT}`: presentation field and outer edge.
- `{GRID_SPEC}` and `{QUADRANT_ORDER}`: exact structure and reading order.
- `{SEASONAL_MOTIF_SETS}`: original allowed shapes for each region.
- `{PALETTE}` and `{NON_COLOR_DISTINCTIONS}`: color and accessible reinforcement.
- `{SEAM_WIDTH}` and `{SYMMETRY_RULE}`: construction geometry.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: square delivery ratio.

## Negative constraints

No copied traditional blocks, ceremonial motifs, broken symmetry, moiré, gradients, color-only meaning, text, brands, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Verify the grid, quadrant order, motif inventory, seam width, and symmetry; confirm each season remains distinct without depending only on color.
