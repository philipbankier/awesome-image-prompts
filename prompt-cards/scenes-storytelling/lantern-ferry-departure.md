# Lantern Ferry Departure

## Use this when

Use this card for one cinematic frame built around a single decisive departure beat. Use a Recipe when character, prop, blocking, and narrative readability require structured inspection.

## Required inputs

Provide the fictional setting, traveler and ferryperson descriptions, exact story beat, prop inventory, blocking, camera, weather, light, palette, aspect ratio, and exclusions.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Stage a fictional lantern-lit ferry landing at {TIME_AND_WEATHER}, viewed from {CAMERA_POSITION} with {LIGHTING_PLAN} separating dock, water, and vessel.
SUBJECT: Show {TRAVELER_DESCRIPTION} performing {DECISIVE_ACTION} while {FERRYPERSON_DESCRIPTION} responds from {FERRY_POSITION}.
DETAILS: Place exactly {PROP_INVENTORY} according to {BLOCKING_PLAN}; preserve {IDENTITY_ANCHORS}, {PALETTE}, coherent reflections, a readable departure direction, and enough negative space around the central gesture.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not add characters, vessels, text, real flags, logos, celebrity likenesses, franchise cues, contradictory light, signatures, or watermarks. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one finished {ASPECT_RATIO} narrative keyframe in which the departure decision reads immediately without a caption.
```

## Variables

- `{TIME_AND_WEATHER}`, `{CAMERA_POSITION}`, and `{LIGHTING_PLAN}`: atmosphere and framing.
- `{TRAVELER_DESCRIPTION}` and `{FERRYPERSON_DESCRIPTION}`: fictional character manifests.
- `{DECISIVE_ACTION}` and `{FERRY_POSITION}`: visible narrative beat.
- `{PROP_INVENTORY}` and `{BLOCKING_PLAN}`: exact objects and spatial relationships.
- `{IDENTITY_ANCHORS}` and `{PALETTE}`: continuity and color rules.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: additional project-specific exclusions.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: delivery crop.

## Negative constraints

No added characters or boats, ambiguous gesture, celebrity likeness, real insignia, franchise design, conflicting reflections, unreadable silhouette, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Identify who is leaving, the direction of departure, and the decisive gesture; count every character and prop; inspect light and reflections for one coherent moment.
