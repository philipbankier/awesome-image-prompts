# Clockwork Seed Heist Triptych

## Use this when

Use this card for a three-panel storyboard with a fixed setup, action, and consequence. It is not for a full comic page or a scene requiring dialogue. Use a Recipe when panel continuity, identity, or repair needs recorded inspection.

## Required inputs

Provide the fictional protagonist and guardian descriptions, exact three-beat sequence, seed capsule and tool inventory, setting, panel widths, camera per panel, palette, aspect ratio, and exclusions.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Build one horizontal three-panel storyboard in {FICTIONAL_VAULT_SETTING}, using {PANEL_WIDTHS} and a consistent {PALETTE}.
SUBJECT: Follow the same {PROTAGONIST_DESCRIPTION} and {GUARDIAN_DESCRIPTION} through the ordered beats in {THREE_BEAT_SEQUENCE}.
DETAILS: Preserve {IDENTITY_ANCHORS}, {SEED_CAPSULE_DESCRIPTION}, and {TOOL_INVENTORY}; apply {CAMERA_PLAN} panel by panel; show setup in panel one, decisive action in panel two, and visible consequence in panel three.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not reorder or repeat beats, change identities or costumes, add panels, dialogue, captions, franchise cues, logos, signatures, or watermarks. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one finished {ASPECT_RATIO} triptych whose story reads left to right without text.
```

## Variables

- `{FICTIONAL_VAULT_SETTING}`, `{PANEL_WIDTHS}`, and `{PALETTE}`: original setting and page system.
- `{PROTAGONIST_DESCRIPTION}` and `{GUARDIAN_DESCRIPTION}`: fictional character manifests.
- `{THREE_BEAT_SEQUENCE}` and `{CAMERA_PLAN}`: exact action and viewpoint order.
- `{IDENTITY_ANCHORS}`, `{SEED_CAPSULE_DESCRIPTION}`, and `{TOOL_INVENTORY}`: continuity details.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: additional project-specific exclusions.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: delivery crop.

## Negative constraints

No fourth panel, reordered action, repeated pose, character drift, dialogue, captions, franchise design, real logos, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Read left to right without prose, match each panel to one supplied beat, and compare character, capsule, and tools across all three panels.
