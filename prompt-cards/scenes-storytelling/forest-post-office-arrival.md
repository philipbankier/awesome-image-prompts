# Forest Post Office Arrival

## Use this when

Use this card for one original picture-book spread with a single arrival beat and one short owned caption. It is not for extending a published children's property. Use a Recipe when exact text, gutter safety, or character continuity requires structured evidence.

## Required inputs

Provide the fictional courier, recipients, forest post office, arrival action, prop and creature inventory, exact caption, gutter position, camera, palette, aspect ratio, and exclusions.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Compose a fictional forest post office across one {ASPECT_RATIO} picture-book spread, viewed from {CAMERA_VIEW}, with the gutter at {GUTTER_POSITION} and {PALETTE} guiding the reading path.
SUBJECT: Show {COURIER_DESCRIPTION} arriving through {ARRIVAL_ACTION} as {RECIPIENT_DESCRIPTION} responds near {POST_OFFICE_DESCRIPTION}.
DETAILS: Include exactly {PROP_AND_CREATURE_INVENTORY}, preserve {BLOCKING_PLAN}, keep all faces and actions clear of the gutter, and render only the caption "{CAPTION_EXACT}" at {CAPTION_POSITION}.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not add characters, dialogue, real postal marks, logos, celebrity likenesses, known children's characters, cropped faces, signatures, or watermarks. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one finished double-page story spread whose arrival reads clearly and whose caption remains legible.
```

## Variables

- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`, `{GUTTER_POSITION}`, and `{PALETTE}`: spread construction.
- `{CAMERA_VIEW}`: approved viewpoint and lens character.
- `{COURIER_DESCRIPTION}` and `{RECIPIENT_DESCRIPTION}`: fictional character manifests.
- `{ARRIVAL_ACTION}` and `{POST_OFFICE_DESCRIPTION}`: story beat and setting.
- `{PROP_AND_CREATURE_INVENTORY}` and `{BLOCKING_PLAN}`: exact content and placement.
- `{CAPTION_EXACT}` and `{CAPTION_POSITION}`: sole approved text and location.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: additional project-specific exclusions.

## Negative constraints

No franchise characters, real postal identity, extra text, added creatures, gutter-obscured faces, celebrity likenesses, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Read the caption character by character, inspect the gutter, count every character and prop, and verify the arrival action without relying on the caption.
