# Storm Shelter Radio Call

## Use this when

Use this card for one suspense frame centered on a fictional ranger making radio contact. It is not for depicting a real emergency service, uniform, disaster, or frequency. Use a Recipe when gesture, weather logic, or repair requires evidence.

## Required inputs

Provide the fictional ranger, civilian role, shelter layout, exact radio action, prop inventory, storm state, camera, light sources, palette, aspect ratio, and exclusions.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Place a fictional storm shelter at {STORM_STATE}, viewed from {CAMERA_POSITION} with {LIGHT_SOURCE_PLAN} revealing the room and weather beyond.
SUBJECT: Show {RANGER_DESCRIPTION} performing {RADIO_ACTION} at {RADIO_POSITION}.
DETAILS: Include exactly {PROP_INVENTORY}, preserve {UNIFORM_MANIFEST}, {BLOCKING_PLAN}, {PALETTE}, readable hand-to-radio contact, wind-consistent debris, and one coherent direction of light.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not add people, real emergency insignia, readable frequencies, brand marks, celebrity likenesses, supernatural effects, extra radios, signatures, or watermarks. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one finished {ASPECT_RATIO} suspense keyframe in which the attempted contact and storm pressure are immediately clear.
```

## Variables

- `{STORM_STATE}`, `{CAMERA_POSITION}`, and `{LIGHT_SOURCE_PLAN}`: weather and framing.
- `{RANGER_DESCRIPTION}` and `{UNIFORM_MANIFEST}`: fictional civilian identity and clothing.
- `{RADIO_ACTION}` and `{RADIO_POSITION}`: exact visible interaction.
- `{PROP_INVENTORY}` and `{BLOCKING_PLAN}`: room content and placement.
- `{PALETTE}` and `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: color and crop.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: additional project-specific exclusions.

## Negative constraints

No real agency marks, frequencies, brand logos, added people, celebrity likenesses, extra radios, impossible weather, contradictory light, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Inspect the hand-radio interaction, count the props, verify wind and lighting directions, and reject any real authority symbol or unexplained character.
