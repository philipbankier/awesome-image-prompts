# Rooftop Garden First Bloom

## Use this when

Use this card for a quiet single-frame discovery in an invented rooftop garden. It is not for depicting a real building, child, or community project. Use a Recipe when identity, blocking, safety, or repair needs recorded review.

## Required inputs

Provide the fictional character, discovery action, exact plant and prop inventory, roof setting, city backdrop, camera, season, light, palette, aspect ratio, and exclusions.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Set a fictional rooftop garden above {CITY_BACKDROP} during {SEASON_AND_TIME}, viewed from {CAMERA_POSITION} under {LIGHT_DESCRIPTION}.
SUBJECT: Show {CHARACTER_DESCRIPTION} discovering {FIRST_BLOOM} through {VISIBLE_ACTION}.
DETAILS: Include exactly {PLANT_AND_PROP_INVENTORY}, preserve {BLOCKING_PLAN}, {PALETTE}, believable planter scale, wind direction, and a focused line of sight between character and bloom.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not add people, logos, readable signs, celebrity likenesses, unsafe roof-edge behavior, impossible plant scale, sentimental effects, signatures, or watermarks. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one finished {ASPECT_RATIO} story frame whose discovery reads before the skyline.
```

## Variables

- `{CITY_BACKDROP}` and `{SEASON_AND_TIME}`: invented context and moment.
- `{CAMERA_POSITION}` and `{LIGHT_DESCRIPTION}`: framing and illumination.
- `{CHARACTER_DESCRIPTION}`, `{FIRST_BLOOM}`, and `{VISIBLE_ACTION}`: fictional subject and beat.
- `{PLANT_AND_PROP_INVENTORY}` and `{BLOCKING_PLAN}`: exact content and positions.
- `{PALETTE}` and `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: visual system and crop.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: additional project-specific exclusions.

## Negative constraints

No real skyline landmark, real person, celebrity likeness, roof-edge danger, added characters, oversized plants, brand marks, signatures, watermarks, or sentimental glow effects.

## Quick check

Follow the character's gaze, verify the single bloom and supplied inventory, inspect scale and roof safety, and reject any real landmark or accidental logo.
