# Paper-Craft Orbit Mobile Concept

## Use this when

Use this card for a handmade paper mobile concept based on abstract orbital forms. Use a Recipe when suspension balance, fabrication dimensions, or installation safety matters.

## Required inputs

- Exact hanging-piece manifest, materials, palette, and relative sizes.
- Suspension structure, cord color, hanging order, and intended movement.
- Background, lighting, camera view, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Create a quiet studio presentation against {BACKGROUND}, viewed from {CAMERA_VIEW} and lit by {LIGHTING} with enough depth to read every hanging layer.

SUBJECT
Show one handmade {MOBILE_NAME} paper-craft mobile suspended from {TOP_STRUCTURE}.

DETAILS
Include exactly {PIECE_MANIFEST}, cut from {PAPER_MATERIALS} in {PALETTE}. Connect the pieces with {CORD_SYSTEM} according to {HANGING_ORDER}, suggesting {MOTION_QUALITY} without motion blur.

CONSTRAINTS
Keep every connection visible, gravity plausible, pieces separated, and the full mobile inside the frame. No real planetary map, scientific labels, extra pieces, broken cords, impossible suspension, plastic gloss, room decor, people, logos, watermarks, letters, or numbers.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one tactile paper-craft concept image at {ASPECT_RATIO}, emphasizing handmade edges, balanced spacing, and a clear orbital rhythm.
```

## Variables

- `{BACKGROUND}`: flat or softly graded studio backdrop.
- `{CAMERA_VIEW}`: approved viewpoint, height, and lens character.
- `{LIGHTING}`: light direction, softness, fill, and shadow detail.
- `{MOBILE_NAME}`: fictional concept name, not necessarily rendered.
- `{TOP_STRUCTURE}`: hoop, dowel, wire armature, or declared support.
- `{PIECE_MANIFEST}`: exact shapes, sizes, and quantities.
- `{PAPER_MATERIALS}`: paper weights, textures, and finishes.
- `{PALETTE}`: closed color list.
- `{CORD_SYSTEM}`: material, color, attachment, and branching.
- `{HANGING_ORDER}`: vertical and horizontal relationship of pieces.
- `{MOTION_QUALITY}`: still, gentle turn, or slight tilt.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: final width-to-height ratio.

## Negative constraints

- No NASA marks, real constellation diagram, text, educational claim, or planet labels.
- No invisible cord, floating piece, tangled line, collision, or unstable support.
- No laser-cut perfection if a handmade finish is requested.

## Quick check

Count the pieces, trace every cord to the support, and verify balance, separation, material texture, and full-frame containment.
