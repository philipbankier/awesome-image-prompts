# Fictional Naturalist Field Guide Spread

## Use this when

Use this card for one illustrated two-page field-guide spread about a deliberately invented plant species, using approved fictional copy and a declared editorial grid. Use the linked [Recipe](../../recipes/fictional-naturalist-field-guide-spread/RECIPE.md) when exact copy, gutter safety, inspection, repair, or evidence matters.

## Required inputs

- Invented common and binomial names with an explicit fiction label.
- Approved morphology, habitat, seasonal cycle, range, and observation notes.
- Exact copy manifest, illustration views, grid, palette, page size, and gutter.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Create one open two-page naturalist field-guide spread on {PAPER_FIELD}, presented flat without a book mockup or surrounding props.

SUBJECT
Document the invented plant {COMMON_NAME_EXACT}, labeled {BINOMIAL_NAME_EXACT}, with {ILLUSTRATION_MANIFEST} as the primary visual evidence.

DETAILS
Arrange {EXACT_COPY_MANIFEST}, {ANNOTATION_LABELS}, {RANGE_PANEL}, and {SEASONAL_PANEL} within {EDITORIAL_GRID}. Use {ILLUSTRATION_STYLE}, {PALETTE}, and {TYPE_DIRECTION}; keep every callout attached to the correct fictional structure.

CONSTRAINTS
Mark the species clearly as fictional. Preserve every supplied name, measurement, label, and note; keep important content outside {GUTTER_AND_MARGIN}. No real taxonomic claim, invented fact beyond the brief, copied plate, pseudo-text, loose label, real publisher mark, signature, watermark, or unapproved copy.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one complete 3:2 field-guide spread at {OUTPUT_SIZE}, with a legible reading order, convincing original botanical illustration, and safe central gutter.
```

## Variables

- `{PAPER_FIELD}`: project-owned paper color and subtle texture.
- `{COMMON_NAME_EXACT}`: exact fictional common name.
- `{BINOMIAL_NAME_EXACT}`: exact invented binomial.
- `{ILLUSTRATION_MANIFEST}`: whole plant, detail views, and scale references.
- `{EXACT_COPY_MANIFEST}`: complete allowed prose and headings.
- `{ANNOTATION_LABELS}`: exact callout labels and target structures.
- `{RANGE_PANEL}`: fictional habitat or range graphic content.
- `{SEASONAL_PANEL}`: approved seasonal stages and labels.
- `{EDITORIAL_GRID}`: columns, panel positions, hierarchy, and reading order.
- `{ILLUSTRATION_STYLE}`: independently specified naturalist technique.
- `{PALETTE}`: approved inks and accent colors.
- `{TYPE_DIRECTION}`: project-owned type character and scale hierarchy.
- `{GUTTER_AND_MARGIN}`: central and outer safe areas.
- `{OUTPUT_SIZE}`: final pixel dimensions.

## Negative constraints

- No real species claim, conservation status, medicinal use, edibility advice, or factual authority beyond the fictional brief.
- No misspelled binomial, detached callout, contradictory anatomy, gutter collision, or unreadable body copy.
- No copied historical plate, publisher logo, page mockup, hand, desk props, signature, or watermark.

## Quick check

Transcribe the names and labels, trace every callout to the correct structure, verify the fiction notice, and inspect the central gutter for clipped text or illustration detail.
