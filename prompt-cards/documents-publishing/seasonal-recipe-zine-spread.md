# Seasonal Recipe Zine Spread

## Use this when

Use this card for one two-page zine spread presenting a tested, approved seasonal recipe with exact quantities, steps, and food imagery. Use a Recipe for nutritional, allergen, food-safety, or print-production review.

## Required inputs

- An approved tested recipe with exact title, yield, time, ingredients, quantities, and ordered steps.
- Approved allergen or safety text, if any.
- Seasonal art direction, food view, grid, page size, gutter, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Create one flat two-page recipe-zine spread on {PAPER_FIELD}, centered on {SEASONAL_CONTEXT} and the finished dish {DISH_DESCRIPTION}.

SUBJECT
Present the exact recipe {RECIPE_TITLE_EXACT}, with yield {YIELD_EXACT}, time {TIME_EXACT}, ingredients {INGREDIENT_MANIFEST}, and steps {METHOD_EXACT}.

DETAILS
Arrange the finished-dish image, {SUPPORTING_ILLUSTRATIONS}, and all text within {EDITORIAL_GRID}. Use {VISUAL_SYSTEM}, {TYPE_DIRECTION}, and {GUTTER_AND_MARGIN}; preserve ingredient-to-step relationships and render only {EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}.

CONSTRAINTS
Do not alter quantities, units, order, temperature, timing, allergen text, or yield. No invented ingredient, missing step, nutrition claim, health claim, unsafe handling advice, pseudo-text, copied food photograph, publisher logo, signature, watermark, or unapproved copy.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one complete 3:2 zine spread at {OUTPUT_SIZE}, with legible recipe text, a safe gutter, and appetizing but plausible project-owned food art direction.
```

## Variables

- `{PAPER_FIELD}`: paper color and restrained texture.
- `{SEASONAL_CONTEXT}`: approved season and ingredient context.
- `{DISH_DESCRIPTION}`: exact appearance and serving treatment.
- `{RECIPE_TITLE_EXACT}`: exact recipe title.
- `{YIELD_EXACT}`: exact approved yield.
- `{TIME_EXACT}`: exact prep and cooking times.
- `{INGREDIENT_MANIFEST}`: exact ingredients, quantities, and units.
- `{METHOD_EXACT}`: exact ordered instructions.
- `{SUPPORTING_ILLUSTRATIONS}`: allowed ingredient or utensil images.
- `{EDITORIAL_GRID}`: column, image, ingredient, and method placement.
- `{VISUAL_SYSTEM}`: project-owned palette, rules, and texture.
- `{TYPE_DIRECTION}`: heading, body, and numeral treatment.
- `{GUTTER_AND_MARGIN}`: central and outer safe areas.
- `{EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}`: complete allowed text list.
- `{OUTPUT_SIZE}`: final pixel dimensions.

## Negative constraints

- No changed quantity, missing unit, reordered step, invented garnish, or contradictory finished dish.
- No medical, dietary, nutrition, allergen, or safety claim beyond approved copy.
- No pseudo-text, cramped body type, gutter collision, cookbook logo, signature, or watermark.

## Quick check

Compare every quantity and step with the approved recipe, confirm the ingredient list supports the pictured dish, and inspect body text and gutter at intended print size.
