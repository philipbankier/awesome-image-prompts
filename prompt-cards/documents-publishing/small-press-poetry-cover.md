# Small-Press Poetry Cover

## Use this when

Use this card for one restrained front cover for a fictional small-press poetry collection using an owned title, byline, and original visual concept. Use a Recipe for full wrap production, licensed poetry, or evidence.

## Required inputs

- Exact project-owned title, author name, and optional press line.
- Original visual motif, emotional register, palette, and typography direction.
- Trim size, bleed intent, output size, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Create one flat front book cover on {BACKGROUND_FIELD}, without a physical book, desk, hand, spine, or retail mockup.

SUBJECT
Build the cover for the fictional poetry collection {TITLE_EXACT} by {AUTHOR_EXACT} around {PRIMARY_MOTIF} and {EMOTIONAL_REGISTER}.

DETAILS
Use {COMPOSITION}, {TYPE_DIRECTION}, {PALETTE}, and {TEXTURE_RULES}. Render only {EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}; keep title and author clearly separated within {TRIM_AND_SAFE_AREA}.

CONSTRAINTS
Preserve every character in the title and byline. No copied cover, quoted poem, real publisher logo, review blurb, award mark, barcode, invented subtitle, stock-photo watermark, decorative pseudo-writing, signature, or unapproved text.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one finished 2:3 front-cover artwork at {OUTPUT_SIZE}, with a memorable original motif and legible title at thumbnail size.
```

## Variables

- `{BACKGROUND_FIELD}`: base color, paper, or abstract field.
- `{TITLE_EXACT}`: exact owned collection title.
- `{AUTHOR_EXACT}`: exact fictional or authorized byline.
- `{PRIMARY_MOTIF}`: one original image or abstract device.
- `{EMOTIONAL_REGISTER}`: intended emotional quality.
- `{COMPOSITION}`: motif, title, byline, and negative-space placement.
- `{TYPE_DIRECTION}`: project-owned typographic character.
- `{PALETTE}`: limited approved colors.
- `{TEXTURE_RULES}`: permitted print or paper texture.
- `{EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}`: complete allowed text list.
- `{TRIM_AND_SAFE_AREA}`: trim, bleed intent, and safe margins.
- `{OUTPUT_SIZE}`: final pixel dimensions.

## Negative constraints

- No copyrighted poem excerpt, real press identity, author likeness, review quote, or award seal.
- No misspelled title, duplicated byline, weak hierarchy, or type outside the safe area.
- No book mockup, spine, barcode, retail sticker, signature, or watermark.

## Quick check

Read the title and byline at thumbnail size, compare them character by character with the manifest, and verify all required type sits inside the declared safe area.
