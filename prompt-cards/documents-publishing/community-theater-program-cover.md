# Community Theater Program Cover

## Use this when

Use this card for one front cover for a fictional community-theater program featuring an original play title and authorized event details. Use a Recipe for a real production, cast likenesses, sponsor obligations, or full program layout.

## Required inputs

- Exact fictional play title, company, venue, dates, and optional season line.
- Original dramatic motif, genre, palette, type direction, and safe area.
- Exact text manifest, output size, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Create one flat portrait program cover for {COMPANY_EXACT} presenting the fictional original play {PLAY_TITLE_EXACT} at {VENUE_EXACT}.

SUBJECT
Build the cover around {DRAMATIC_MOTIF}, conveying {GENRE_AND_TONE} without depicting a recognizable performer or copying known stage imagery.

DETAILS
Use {COMPOSITION}, {VISUAL_SYSTEM}, and {TYPE_DIRECTION}. Render only {EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}; keep title, company, venue, and dates in the declared hierarchy {TYPE_HIERARCHY}, with all content inside {SAFE_AREA}.

CONSTRAINTS
Preserve spelling, dates, punctuation, and line breaks. No real play title, production logo, actor likeness, critic quote, award, sponsor, ticketing mark, fake credit block, pseudo-text, mockup, signature, watermark, or unapproved copy.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one finished 2:3 program-cover artwork at {OUTPUT_SIZE}, with an original theatrical motif and all event information readable at hand-held size.
```

## Variables

- `{COMPANY_EXACT}`: exact fictional theater company.
- `{PLAY_TITLE_EXACT}`: exact fictional original-play title.
- `{VENUE_EXACT}`: exact fictional venue.
- `{DRAMATIC_MOTIF}`: one project-owned visual concept.
- `{GENRE_AND_TONE}`: emotional and theatrical direction.
- `{COMPOSITION}`: motif and text placement.
- `{VISUAL_SYSTEM}`: project-owned palette, geometry, and texture.
- `{TYPE_DIRECTION}`: independently specified lettering character.
- `{EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}`: complete allowed strings.
- `{TYPE_HIERARCHY}`: ordered text levels.
- `{SAFE_AREA}`: minimum inset for all text and critical artwork.
- `{OUTPUT_SIZE}`: final pixel dimensions.

## Negative constraints

- No existing play, theater company, production mark, poster composition, or performer likeness.
- No misspelled title, conflicting date, invented sponsor, decorative pseudo-writing, or clipped type.
- No booklet mockup, paper folds, ticket stub, signature, or watermark.

## Quick check

Transcribe every visible string, confirm the fictional title leads the hierarchy, and verify the motif does not resemble a known production or identifiable performer.
