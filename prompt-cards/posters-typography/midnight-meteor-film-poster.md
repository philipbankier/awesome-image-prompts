# Midnight Meteor Film Poster

## Use this when

Use this card for one fictional cinematic poster driven by a single meteor-night concept and a short exact billing manifest. Use a Recipe for real film marketing, likeness use, legal billing, or evidence.

## Required inputs

- Fictional film title, tagline, release line, and optional credit block.
- Central scene, genre, emotional tone, palette, and typography direction.
- Exact text, poster size, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Create one portrait poster for the fictional {GENRE} film {TITLE_EXACT}, set at {LOCATION} during {METEOR_EVENT}.

SUBJECT
Feature {CENTRAL_SUBJECT} beneath one dominant meteor path, conveying {EMOTIONAL_TONE} without showing a recognizable real person.

DETAILS
Use {COMPOSITION}, {PALETTE_AND_LIGHT}, and {TYPE_DIRECTION}. Render only {EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}, with the title first, tagline second, and release line third.

CONSTRAINTS
Keep the meteor physically coherent, the silhouette original, and the title fully legible. No actor likeness, franchise imagery, real studio mark, copied key art, invented review quote, extra planet, duplicated figure, pseudo-text, signature, watermark, or unapproved copy.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one finished 2:3 film poster at {OUTPUT_SIZE}, with cinematic scale, a clean silhouette, and a memorable title treatment.
```

## Variables

- `{GENRE}`: fictional film genre.
- `{TITLE_EXACT}`: exact fictional title.
- `{LOCATION}`: original landscape or built setting.
- `{METEOR_EVENT}`: sky direction, scale, and light behavior.
- `{CENTRAL_SUBJECT}`: non-identifiable subject or object.
- `{EMOTIONAL_TONE}`: intended mood.
- `{COMPOSITION}`: camera view, horizon, figure scale, and negative space.
- `{PALETTE_AND_LIGHT}`: limited colors and coherent sources.
- `{TYPE_DIRECTION}`: project-owned lettering character and placement.
- `{EXACT_TEXT_MANIFEST}`: complete allowed strings.
- `{OUTPUT_SIZE}`: final pixel dimensions.

## Negative constraints

- No recognizable performer, real film property, studio logo, festival laurel, or critic attribution.
- No multiple meteors unless declared, malformed silhouette, unreadable title, or conflicting light direction.
- No fake credit noise, decorative pseudo-writing, mockup frame, signature, or watermark.

## Quick check

Confirm one original focal silhouette, one declared meteor event, a readable title, and no visible text beyond the exact fictional manifest.
