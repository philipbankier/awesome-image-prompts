# Seasonal Letterform Specimen

## Use this when

Use this card for one square display-lettering specimen that explores a fictional seasonal alphabet through exact approved characters and a restrained material motif. Use a Recipe for font engineering, complete character-set validation, or commercial type licensing.

## Required inputs

- Exact character set and specimen words.
- Season, material motif, letter construction rules, and hierarchy.
- Palette, grid, output size, and aspect ratio.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE
Create one square letterform specimen on {BACKGROUND}, inspired by {SEASONAL_CONDITIONS} and {MATERIAL_MOTIF} without depicting a full landscape.

SUBJECT
Show exactly {CHARACTER_SET} and {SPECIMEN_WORDS} as a coherent project-authored display alphabet built from {LETTER_CONSTRUCTION}.

DETAILS
Arrange the characters in {GRID_AND_HIERARCHY}, using {PALETTE}, {STROKE_RULES}, and {DETAIL_RULES}. Keep baseline, cap height, spacing, repeated forms, and stroke logic visually consistent.

CONSTRAINTS
Preserve every approved character and word exactly. No extra alphabet, lorem ipsum, copied typeface specimen, named designer imitation, illegible ornament, photographic mockup, logo, signature, watermark, or unapproved text.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one 1:1 letterform specimen at {OUTPUT_SIZE}, with enough scale to compare character construction and enough seasonal character to feel distinctive.
```

## Variables

- `{BACKGROUND}`: flat or subtly textured project-owned field.
- `{SEASONAL_CONDITIONS}`: one season and its light or weather cues.
- `{MATERIAL_MOTIF}`: independently specified natural or fabricated material.
- `{CHARACTER_SET}`: exact letters, numerals, and punctuation.
- `{SPECIMEN_WORDS}`: exact approved display words.
- `{LETTER_CONSTRUCTION}`: modular shape logic.
- `{GRID_AND_HIERARCHY}`: placement, scale levels, and reading order.
- `{PALETTE}`: approved color list.
- `{STROKE_RULES}`: weight, contrast, terminals, and joins.
- `{DETAIL_RULES}`: permitted seasonal details and limits.
- `{OUTPUT_SIZE}`: final pixel dimensions.

## Negative constraints

- No copied commercial font, real foundry branding, font name, or named-designer imitation.
- No missing character, substituted glyph, inconsistent baseline, or uncontrolled decorative branch.
- No paragraph filler, UI chrome, mockup context, signature, or watermark.

## Quick check

Count and transcribe every requested character, compare repeated strokes and terminals, and verify that seasonal details do not obstruct glyph recognition.
