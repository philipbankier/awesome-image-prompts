COMMISSION
Create one flat 4:5 poster announcing the fictional Small Worlds Miniature Fair. This is an independently authored visual concept: a tiny pavilion sits inside an oversized measuring grid.

CONTENT
The fictional event invites hobbyists and model makers to a one-day fair at the invented Juniper Hall. Render only this exact approved copy, preserving spelling and hierarchy:
SMALL WORLDS
MINIATURE FAIR
JUNIPER HALL
SATURDAY 14 SEPTEMBER
Do not render any other text, measurements, numerals, labels, prices, sponsors, claims, signatures, or marks.

VISUAL SYSTEM
Use one jewel-like paper pavilion, visibly tiny, centered low inside a huge pale-blue measuring grid. Let the surrounding empty field dramatically communicate scale. Palette: soft ivory, cobalt blue, coral red, and moss green. Typography direction: original compact uppercase drafting letters with softly rounded terminals. Place SMALL WORLDS high and spacious; tuck the event type, venue, and date neatly along one bold ruler edge near the bottom. Keep an 8% safe margin, a clear reading path, clean grid geometry, no text on measurement marks, and no clipping.

CONSTRAINTS
Exactly one tiny pavilion. The grid and ruler contain graphic ticks only, no numbers or labels. No people, toys from known brands, copied architecture, logos, pseudo-writing, watermark, mockup, frame, wall, hands, or extra option.

OUTPUT INTENT
Return one flat 1024x1280 pixel miniature-fair poster in 4:5 portrait orientation.
