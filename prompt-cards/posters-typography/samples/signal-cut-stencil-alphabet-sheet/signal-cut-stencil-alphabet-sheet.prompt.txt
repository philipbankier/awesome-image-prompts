Typography direction: an original uppercase industrial grotesk, wide and sturdy, with uniform angled bridge cuts; numerals and punctuation match the same system. Organize uppercase rows on precise shared baselines, with numerals and punctuation below. Keep an 8% clean safe margin, strict left-to-right reading order, evenly spaced cells, no clipped glyphs, and the title clearly subordinate to the alphabet.

No pseudo-writing. Use only this fictional project-authored concept. No real brand, copied typeface artwork, logo, watermark, mockup, frame, wall, hands, or extra design option.
