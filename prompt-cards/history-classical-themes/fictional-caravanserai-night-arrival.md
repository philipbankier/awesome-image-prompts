# Fictional Caravanserai Night Arrival

## Use this when

Use this card for an invented premodern arrival scene constrained to one approved place and period. It is not for blending unrelated cultures or treating trade-route life as exotic spectacle. Use a Recipe when cultural review, fact mapping, or repair must be recorded.

## Required inputs

Provide the region, century range, caravanserai architecture, traveler count and roles, animal and gear inventory, arrival action, clothing, weather, camera, light, palette, aspect ratio, and cultural review notes.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Stage a fictional caravanserai courtyard in {REGION_AND_DATE_RANGE} at {TIME_AND_WEATHER}, viewed from {CAMERA_POSITION} under {LIGHTING_PLAN}.
SUBJECT: Show exactly {TRAVELER_COUNT} fictional travelers in {TRAVELER_ROLES}, completing {ARRIVAL_ACTION} beside {ARCHITECTURAL_FEATURES}.
DETAILS: Include only {ANIMAL_AND_GEAR_INVENTORY}, {CLOTHING_MANIFEST}, and {TRADE_GOODS}; use {PALETTE}, plausible scale, grounded animal handling, and spatial relationships approved in {CULTURAL_REVIEW_NOTES}.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not mix regions or centuries, invent sacred symbols, exaggerate wealth or danger, sexualize or exoticize people, add modern equipment, famous figures, logos, signatures, or watermarks.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one finished {ASPECT_RATIO} fictional historical scene with a legible arrival action and respectful material detail.
```

## Variables

- `{REGION_AND_DATE_RANGE}` and `{TIME_AND_WEATHER}`: approved scope and moment.
- `{CAMERA_POSITION}` and `{LIGHTING_PLAN}`: framing and illumination.
- `{TRAVELER_COUNT}`, `{TRAVELER_ROLES}`, and `{ARRIVAL_ACTION}`: exact people, their assigned roles, and visible event.
- `{ARCHITECTURAL_FEATURES}` and `{ANIMAL_AND_GEAR_INVENTORY}`: closed setting inventory.
- `{CLOTHING_MANIFEST}`, `{TRADE_GOODS}`, and `{CULTURAL_REVIEW_NOTES}`: factual and cultural boundaries.
- `{PALETTE}` and `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: visual and delivery rules.

## Negative constraints

No culture or period mixing, invented sacred symbols, stereotypes, exoticizing treatment, modern equipment, famous figures, logos, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Count travelers and animals, map every visible object to the approved inventory, and check for mixed periods, regions, or stereotyped treatment.
