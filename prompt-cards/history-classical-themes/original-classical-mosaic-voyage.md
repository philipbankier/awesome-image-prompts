# Original Classical Mosaic Voyage

## Use this when

Use this card for a newly designed mosaic-style panel using a general historical technique. It is not for recreating an extant mosaic or named archaeological work. Use a Recipe when inventory, period review, or repair must be recorded.

## Required inputs

Provide the fictional voyage subject, vessel type, figure and creature counts, border geometry, tessera palette, surface wear, aspect ratio, and excluded real artifacts.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Present one flat mosaic panel on {STONE_FIELD}, bounded by {ORIGINAL_BORDER_GEOMETRY} and viewed straight on.
SUBJECT: Depict the fictional voyage {VOYAGE_ACTION} using {VESSEL_DESCRIPTION}, exactly {FIGURE_COUNT}, and exactly {CREATURE_COUNT}.
DETAILS: Construct the image from {TESSERA_PALETTE}, consistent tessera scale, period-plausible wave and drapery abstractions, {SURFACE_WEAR}, and a newly composed silhouette system.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not reproduce any named or extant mosaic, inscription, deity portrait, ruler, modern ship feature, logo, signature, watermark, or photorealistic depth. Also avoid {MUST_AVOID}.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one original {ASPECT_RATIO} mosaic-style decorative panel shown as a complete, inspectable object.
```

## Variables

- `{STONE_FIELD}` and `{ORIGINAL_BORDER_GEOMETRY}`: backing and project-authored frame.
- `{VOYAGE_ACTION}` and `{VESSEL_DESCRIPTION}`: fictional subject and craft.
- `{FIGURE_COUNT}` and `{CREATURE_COUNT}`: exact counts.
- `{TESSERA_PALETTE}` and `{SURFACE_WEAR}`: material and age treatment.
- `{MUST_AVOID}`: excluded real artifacts and other prohibited motifs.
- `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: output crop.

## Negative constraints

No copied archaeological work, real inscription, named figure, modern vessel detail, photorealism, text, logos, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Count figures and creatures, inspect tessera scale and vessel details, and compare the composition against the excluded-artifact list.
