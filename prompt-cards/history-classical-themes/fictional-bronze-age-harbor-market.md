# Fictional Bronze Age Harbor Market

## Use this when

Use this card for a clearly labeled fictional composite constrained by an approved Bronze Age fact pack. Use a Recipe when source mapping, anachronism review, and repair must be recorded.

## Required inputs

Provide the defined region and century range, source-owner-approved architecture, vessels, clothing, goods, tools, activities, exclusions, camera, weather, light, palette, aspect ratio, and fictional-composite label.

## Prompt

```text
SCENE: Reconstruct a clearly fictional composite harbor market for {REGION_AND_DATE_RANGE}, viewed from {CAMERA_POSITION} during {WEATHER_AND_LIGHT}.
SUBJECT: Center the approved market activity {PRIMARY_ACTIVITY} among {ARCHITECTURE_AND_VESSELS}.
DETAILS: Include only {CLOTHING_MANIFEST}, {GOODS_AND_TOOLS}, and {SECONDARY_ACTIVITIES}; follow {MATERIAL_CULTURE_RULES}, {PALETTE}, plausible human scale, and one coherent shoreline layout.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not add objects, scripts, weapons, architecture, animals, clothing, or technologies outside {FACT_PACK_BOUNDARY}. Do not copy a known artifact, depict a named ruler, claim documentary certainty, or include logos, signatures, and watermarks.
OUTPUT INTENT: Return one finished {ASPECT_RATIO} historical-concept image labeled in accompanying metadata as a fictional composite, not direct evidence.
```

## Variables

- `{REGION_AND_DATE_RANGE}` and `{FACT_PACK_BOUNDARY}`: approved temporal and geographic scope.
- `{CAMERA_POSITION}` and `{WEATHER_AND_LIGHT}`: framing and atmosphere.
- `{PRIMARY_ACTIVITY}` and `{SECONDARY_ACTIVITIES}`: exact visible actions.
- `{ARCHITECTURE_AND_VESSELS}`: approved built and maritime forms.
- `{CLOTHING_MANIFEST}`, `{GOODS_AND_TOOLS}`, and `{MATERIAL_CULTURE_RULES}`: closed period inventory.
- `{PALETTE}` and `{ASPECT_RATIO}`: visual and delivery rules.

## Negative constraints

No anachronistic technology, mixed centuries or regions, copied artifacts, named historical figures, modern text, false certainty, exoticizing treatment, signatures, or watermarks.

## Quick check

Map every visible object and activity to the fact pack, inspect scale and shoreline logic, and reject anything outside the approved period or region.
