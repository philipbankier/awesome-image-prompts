AUTHORITATIVE FACTS
- Consider 1,000 components: 100 are defective and 900 are good.
- The sensor flags 90 of the 100 defective components.
- The sensor flags 45 of the 900 good components.
- The flagged group contains 135 components: 90 defective and 45 good.
- P(defective) = 10%.
- P(flagged | defective) = 90%.
- P(flagged | good) = 5%.
- P(flagged) = 135 / 1,000 = 13.5%.
- P(defective | flagged) = 90 / 135 = 2 / 3 ≈ 66.7%.
- Bayes' theorem is P(D | +) = P(+ | D)P(D) / P(+).

REQUIRED RELATIONSHIPS
- Split 1,000 components into 100 defective and 900 good.
- From the defective branch, 90 enter the flagged group. From the good branch, 45 enter the flagged group.
- The posterior denominator is the full flagged group of 135, not the 100 defective components.
- Substitute 90%, 10%, and 13.5% into Bayes' theorem to obtain approximately 66.7%.
